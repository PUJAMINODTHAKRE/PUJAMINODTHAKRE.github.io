Research question

How does the choice of knowledge representation technique influence the reasoning performance and answer quality of expert systems in the healthcare domain?

The benchmark was intentionally designed so that query complexity increases from Boolean conditions toward quantified, relational, multi-relational, and contextual reasoning.
Representation paradigms
Propositional Logic · First-Order Predicate Logic · Rule-Based Systems · Relational Databases · Frame-Based Models · Ontologies · Knowledge Graphs
Evaluation dimensions
Semantic expressiveness · Query coverage · Explainability · Scalability. The main published comparative results emphasize query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability.
Core reasoning concept
Reasoning depth captures the inferential distance required to derive an answer. For graph-based reasoning, the study defines it through the minimum valid inference-path length.
Benchmark

Five clinical queries

The query set progressively stresses the expressive limits of each representation.

Q1 · Boolean

“Is a patient at risk of heart disease if chest pain is present and smoking history is negative?”

Tests explicit Boolean conditions.

Q2 · Universal / quantified

“Are all patients over 60 years old with hypertension at elevated risk of cardiovascular disease?”

Introduces a quantified condition over a group of patients.

Q3 · Relational

“What are the cardiovascular risks for patients with both diabetes and obesity?”

Requires relationships among multiple patient attributes.

Q4 · Multi-relational

“Which treatment guidelines are applicable for diabetic patients with left ventricular hypertrophy and a history of atrial fibrillation?”

Connects multiple clinical conditions, patient history, and treatment guidance.

Q5 · Contextual

“Suggest interventions for patients with comorbidities similar to those reported in recent literature.”

Introduces contextual integration beyond directly encoded patient facts.

02 · Ongoing collaborative research

Cryptocurrency Markets & Geopolitical Risk

2025–present

High-frequency event study

Geopolitical shocks in financial markets

Collaborative research examining the response of cryptocurrency and traditional financial markets to geopolitical shocks during the Israel–Gaza conflict.

High-Frequency DataGeopolitical RiskStatistical AnalysisFinancial Markets

Research components

The study integrates time-stamped geopolitical news with hourly financial data and develops an Hourly War Risk Index (HWRI) to distinguish escalation and de-escalation shocks.

Data & event construction
3,395 geopolitical reports were consolidated into 2,835 unique events for analysis.
Event-study measures
Abnormal Returns (AR), Cumulative Abnormal Returns (CAR), and Cumulative Average Abnormal Returns (CAAR) were estimated across 1–48 hour event windows.
Reported result pattern
The reported results indicate heterogeneous and short-lived asset-specific responses, with no persistent market-wide relationship between war risk and abnormal returns over the full study period.
