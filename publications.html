“Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms.”

Atul Kumar Tripathi, Puja Minodji Thakre, and Niladri Chatterjee. Intelligent Computing: Proceedings of the 2026 Computing Conference, Volume 2, Lecture Notes in Networks and Information Systems, vol. 1950, pp. 38–54, Springer, 2026.
Open DOI ↗Research repository ↗
Publication context

Research contribution

The paper presents a unified comparative framework for knowledge representation in healthcare question answering. The study uses 47 cardiovascular patient records and five clinical queries and evaluates the representations through query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability.

Research object

The comparison spans formal and graph-oriented representations, allowing the same underlying healthcare knowledge and benchmark queries to be expressed in different forms and examined under increasing reasoning complexity.

Published result highlighted on this site

Knowledge Graphs achieved complete benchmark coverage and supported multi-hop and contextual reasoning with path-based explainability within the study benchmark.
