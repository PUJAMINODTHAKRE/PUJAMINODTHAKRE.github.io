Research record

What the paper studies

A unified empirical comparison of knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare Question Answering, using a cardiovascular benchmark of 47 patient records and five benchmark queries.

Knowledge RepresentationKnowledge GraphsHealthcare QAExplainabilityReasoning DepthRDF / OWL / SPARQL
Abstract-level summary

Research contribution

Comparative framework

The work establishes a common evaluation framework for multiple KR paradigms and examines their reasoning capabilities under progressively more complex clinical queries.

Empirical benchmark

The benchmark consists of 47 cardiovascular patient records and five clinical queries designed to move from Boolean conditions to contextual reasoning.

Evaluation

The study evaluates query coverage, reasoning depth, explainability, semantic expressiveness, and broader scalability considerations.

Published result

Within the experimental benchmark, Knowledge Graphs answered all five benchmark queries and supported multi-hop, contextual, and path-based reasoning traces.
