Springer · Intelligent Computing · LNNS volume 1950 · 2026

Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms

Atul Kumar Tripathi, Puja Minodji Thakre, and Niladri Chatterjee. Intelligent Computing: Proceedings of the 2026 Computing Conference, Volume 2, Lecture Notes in Networks and Information Systems, vol. 1950, pp. 38–54, Springer, 2026.

Official DOI ↗Research repository ↗
Contribution

What the work studies

The paper develops a unified evaluation framework for comparing multiple knowledge-representation paradigms in a common healthcare question-answering setting.

The benchmark contains 47 cardiovascular patient records and five questions designed to cover Boolean, relational, multi-relational and contextual reasoning.

Evaluation

What is measured

Query coverage — whether each benchmark query can be answered.

Reasoning depth — the number of inferential steps needed for a valid answer.

Explainability — how the answer can be traced or justified within the representation.
