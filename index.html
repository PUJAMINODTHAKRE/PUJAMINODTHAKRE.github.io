About

A mathematics-trained researcher building toward intelligent systems.

My academic training is rooted in mathematics, with postgraduate work at IIT Delhi and a growing research focus on statistical learning, data mining, structured knowledge and intelligent information systems.

My research experience combines two complementary modes of inquiry: representing complex domain knowledge so that machines can retrieve and reason over it, and using statistical methods to study how real-world information interacts with observed data.

The common thread is methodological: making relationships explicit, evaluating them carefully, and keeping the reasoning process interpretable. My current work brings together knowledge representation, knowledge graphs, NLP, statistical learning and quantitative analysis.
